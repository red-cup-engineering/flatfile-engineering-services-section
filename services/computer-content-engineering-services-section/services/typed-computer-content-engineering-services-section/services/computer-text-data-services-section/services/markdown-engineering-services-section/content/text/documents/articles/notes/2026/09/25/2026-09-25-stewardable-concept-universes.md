# Eight Stewardable Concept Universes

The legacy proposal's only observable effect is a research map: it groups
possible application content into eight territories and gives each a proposed
operator family. It supplies no implementation, data schema, network
protocol, or demonstrated algebra. The 2026 snapshot has seven domain
documents and an overview; Runtime & Orchestration is named in that overview
without its own domain document.

| Territory | Proposed operators | Scope cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relational Agency & Governance | Observe, Act, Evaluate | identity, delegation, consent, quorum, accountability |
| Knowledge Fabric | Recognize, Relate, Prove, Publish | language, semantic frames, proofs, documentation |
| Measurement & Analytics | Sense, Aggregate, Evaluate, Alert | signals, calibration, tests, drift, reports |
| Runtime & Orchestration | Plan, Deploy, Execute, Repair | interpreters, jobs, safety envelopes, compensation |
| Resource, Storage & Replication | Allocate, Persist, Replicate, Catalog | capacity, retention, replicas, provenance |
| Productivity & Operations | Plan, Execute, Stabilize, Audit | schedules, tasks, runbooks, logistics |
| Media, Experience & Creativity | Compose, Curate, Deliver, Record | rights, accessibility, curation, attribution |
| Simulation, Science & Topology | Model, Simulate, Observe, Interact | experiments, constraints, local-to-global models, interactive worlds |

The repeated proposal was a complete Heyting carrier `H_X`, a domain-law
closure `j`, a temporal trace `G`, finite fragment generators, failure
residues, and cross-territory morphisms. The relevant mathematical program is
recorded in [The Stewardable Semiotic Concept Universe](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/philosophy-services-section/services/relationality-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/stewardable-semiotic-concept-universe.md).
This scope map does not establish that any territory has that structure, that
the listed operator names form a common algebra, or that `j` and `G` coincide
with any platform closure or event-log operation.

The platform is instead organized by actual carriers and dependency direction:
data, code, networking, then systems. An application sublocale may use this
map to name its own domain vocabulary, but each concrete capability must state
its data, transformation, boundary, and observable effect there. The map
cannot create a shared service, authorize an operation, or partition platform
architecture.
