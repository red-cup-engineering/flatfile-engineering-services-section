# MASH Legacy Capability Disposition

The MASH snapshots (`mash`, `2026-02-24-mash`,
`2026-03-11-mash-library`, and `2026-03-11-mash-rust-engine-pre-pam`) are not
an executable knowledge-system server. The later two source trees are
byte-for-byte duplicates of fragments inside the 2026-02-24 archive. That
engine declares algebra, inference, and proof modules, but none is present in
the archive or its retained Git history; it also has no workspace manifest.
The pre-PAM variant separately lacks its declared `src/main.rs`, dashboard,
and MUD modules. The remaining Unix-socket hub accepts only a three-string
assertion and turns every assertion into positive evidence. Its static
ontology agents and stateful projection ports consequently cannot provide the
advertised generic cascade.

The scientific proposal survives as a draft, not as a conclusion established
by this archive. [The Adjoint Structure of Predication on Bilattice-Valued
Fibers](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/information-science-services-section/services/math-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/research-articles/the-adjoint-structure-of-predication-on-bilattice-valued-fibers.md)
describes the FOUR twist product, fusion/residuum adjunction, and
predicate-fiber interpretation. The observed SignChain carrier realizes the
finite algebra: its four points satisfy all 64 fusion/residuum cases.

The surviving implementation is separated by carrier:

- SignCell owns a triple and its two grow-only evidence sets as data.
- SignChain reads and transforms those tellings: two orders, FOUR, fusion,
  residuum, and knowledge join as code.
- EAVT owns fsynced append-only rows; flatfile-resource-graph witness replay
  validates witness identity and causality before deterministically folding a
  journal.
- Typed JSNode command execution owns exact program execution and receipts.
  Raw shell, container, and Claude adapters have no authority without an
  admitted operation supplied by a system.
- FeltOS owns the MUD's stateful world and Telnet server; Wheatstone is its
  terminal client. They are applications, not general evidence infrastructure.

A content-addressed payload remains content data, so MASH's inline/SHA-256
blob split does not survive as a protocol. Its JSON-on-Unix-socket protocol,
hand-written Markdown parser, static OQRA boot claims, greedy permanent
dispatch selection, and unimplemented "five morphisms" do not survive: none
has an independently usable carrier, and retaining them would create a second
inference or system owner.
