# The standard-document-release cycle (this node's law)

One iteration, run against the Leader Movement Team's demand
(`emsenn/requirement/automated-idea-to-standard-document-release-circuit`):

1. **Name the token.** A semantic token — a Leader-admitted framing, a term
   cluster, a process design kit — is judged unique and rich enough in the
   corpus that packaging it as a standards-form document is now warranted.
   The judgment and its evidence are named in the tracking work order's
   `demand`, never asserted only in this node's own record.
2. **Collect real sources.** Every corpus path the token actually lives at —
   notes, glossary terms, running code, prior work orders — is read and
   listed by exact path before composition starts. A source that cannot be
   pointed at by path is not cited.
3. **Compose faithfully.** The sources are composed into one IEEE/RFC/arxiv-
   style document: Scope, Normative References, Definitions, the paradigm's
   normative content, Conformance. Every normative-content section names the
   path(s) it was drawn from. No section states a claim its cited sources do
   not support; a thesis-grade source stays thesis-grade in the draft.
4. **Mark and hold.** The draft is saved under this node's own
   `content/documents/specifications/` tree, marked `DRAFT-FOR-LEADER-REVIEW`
   in its front matter and body. It is not published, not pushed to GitHub,
   and not represented anywhere as accepted.
5. **Track exact evidence.** One LMT work order of class `define` is
   minted against the draft's path. Draft evidence binds exact source,
   transformation, result, attribution, and replay material; the distinct
   acceptance disposition remains the Leader's or the Leader's delegate's.
   Producer identity neither proves nor disproves the draft's correctness.

Win condition: a semantic token that has become unique and rich enough moves
from corpus prose to a reviewable standards-form draft without emsenn having
to notice it and route it by hand — the idea's own ask.

Not granted: publishing a draft externally under any license; representing a
draft as accepted, seated, or Leader-approved; composing a normative section
from a source this node cannot cite by path; upgrading a thesis-grade corpus
claim to a proven one inside the draft.
