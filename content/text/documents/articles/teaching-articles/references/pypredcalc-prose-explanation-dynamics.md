## Realized

- `SubstitutionMatrix` — integer-matrix substitution dynamical systems.
  Determinant (Bareiss), primitivity, Perron eigenvalue (numerical via Durand–Kerner), Pisot test, regime classification, Smith invariants of the transpose, per-letter d-frame shadow accounting and self-duality test.
- Named instances: `FIBONACCI`, `THUE_MORSE`, `PADOVAN`, `TRIBONACCI`, `TETRANACCI` plus their default bilattice valuations.

## Stubs

- `settling` — `objects/predication-settling*.md`, `lorentzian-causal-settling`, `stochastic-settling`, `continuous-valued-settling`, etc.
- `cosettling` — transfinite generalizations of the strict-regime closure-rule cosettling that lives in `pypred.combinatorics.closure_rule`.
