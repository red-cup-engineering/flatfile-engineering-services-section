## Realized

- `alexandrov` — finite Alexandrov topology on a preorder; the bijection between preorders on a finite set and Alexandrov topologies on it is exposed via `AlexandrovTopology` and the inverse `specialisation_preorder`.
  Corpus: `objects/alexandrov-topology.md`.
- `priestley` — finite Birkhoff duality between bounded distributive lattices and finite posets, via `priestley_dual` and `lattice_from_poset`, with carriers `FiniteDistributiveLattice` and `FinitePriestleySpace`.
  Corpus: `objects/priestley-space.md`, `paired-priestley-space`, `pairwise-stone-space`, `priestley-dual-cover-topology`.
