## Realized

- `cohomology_rank`, `coboundary_matrix` — Z-coefficient cellular cohomology over Q (free part of H^n).
- `ConstantSheaf` — constant Z-stalk sheaf on a simplicial complex.
- `CellularSheaf` — Z-valued cellular sheaf with arbitrary integer restrictions (currently rank-1 stalks only).
- `FrameValuedSheaf` — PowersetFrame stalks; H⁰ via global-section enumeration; H₀ of cosheaf side via union-find on right-adjoint identifications.
