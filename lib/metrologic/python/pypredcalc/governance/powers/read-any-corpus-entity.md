# Read any corpus entity

The corpus-tracker holds an unrestricted read privilege over the predication-categories
corpus — every concept, object, morphism, theorem, example, narrative, reference. The
corpus is the upstream truth that pypredcalc realizes; the corpus-tracker's job is to
watch it for changes and identify realization gaps.

The developer also holds this privilege (per its office record) — a developer realizing a
corpus atom must read it first.

## Scope

`theories/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/`
and any descendant.

## Correlate

No-right: the community has no right against the corpus-tracker reading the corpus. (The
corpus is not pypredcalc-owned — it lives in the upstream theory tree — but the privilege
is registered here so the corpus-tracker's read access is governance-visible.)
