# PyPredCalc personnel

Personnel records at pypredcalc-scope. The personnel themselves are recognized at the
polity layer ([`561/governance/personnel/`](../../../../../governance/personnel/)); this
directory carries the **incumbency cells** that bind a polity-personnel into a
pypredcalc-scope office.

Per [`office-as-double-settlement`](../../../../../theories/theories/humanities/theories/math/theories/categories/theories/predication-categories/objects/),
incumbency requires two predicates settled simultaneously:

- **Recognition** (backward-sensitive): the community knows this personnel as legitimately
  holding the office.
- **Induction** (forward-obligating): the personnel has accepted the office's duties.

The steward exercises [`install-personnel`](../powers/install-personnel.md) to settle
both. Until installation, an entity acting in an office's role does so informally — the
substrate has no incumbency cells settled.

## Pypredcalc-scope incumbency records

|---|---|---|
| [`emsenn`](../../../../../governance/personnel/emsenn.md) | [`emsenn-incumbency`](emsenn-incumbency.md) | All 17 offices (informal); see record. |

## Note on informal incumbency

All current incumbency is informal — the captain (`emsenn`) holds every office until
substrate-driven dispatch (the `pyfars run pypredcalc/` operational target) makes
substrate-readable incumbency load-bearing. At that point, the steward will exercise
[`install-personnel`](../powers/install-personnel.md) explicitly per office, settling the
recognition + induction cells in the substrate. New personnel (additional contracted
contributors, federated polity members) get their own incumbency records here.
