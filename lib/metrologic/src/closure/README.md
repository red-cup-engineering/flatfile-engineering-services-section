# @red-cup-engineering/metrologic/closure

**Finite closure operators over evidence: formal concept analysis and directed evidence closure.**

- **formal-context** — the formal context and its Galois machinery: extents, intents, attribute closure, the Duquenne–Guigues canonical basis (Ganter's algorithm), and the denial lane (attribute alphabet M ⊔ ¬M).
- **formal-concepts** — enumeration of a context's formal concepts.
- **directed-evidence** — finite directed evidence closure, a powerset-stalk realization of TarskiSheafClosure's directed `driveFn`.

The closure ordinal κ, its transfinite approximants and the four Obsmetrologia regimes (strict, scott, transfinite-bounded, aperiodic) are not here: they live in the one closure engine, `@red-cup-engineering/metrologic/articulation/predicating/positing/settling/closure` (`closureProcess`, `closeOrdinalProcess`, `closureRegimeOfOrdinal`), whose regimes are read off the observed ordinal and the process's Scott-continuity witness.
